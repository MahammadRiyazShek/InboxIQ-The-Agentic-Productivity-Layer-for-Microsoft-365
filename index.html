Multi-Agent Roles

Seven specialists. One coordinated team.

Powered by AutoGen v0.4 group-chat. Each agent owns one job and they collaborate through a shared planner-critic loop.

Agent 01

Planner

Decomposes the user's high-level goal into sub-tasks and dispatches them to the right specialist.

Agent 02

Classifier

Tags every email with priority + intent in <200ms using GPT-4o-mini.

Agent 03

Drafter

Writes replies in your tone, grounded by RAG over your last 90 days of correspondence.

Agent 04

Scheduler

Finds slots, resolves conflicts, and books meetings via Microsoft Graph Calendar.

Agent 05

Critic

Validates every outbound action against your policy DSL and content-safety filters.

Agent 06

Reporter

Compiles your morning briefing — text + 60-second voice audio via Azure Speech.
